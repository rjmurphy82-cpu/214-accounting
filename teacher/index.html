Chapter 10Bonds and long-term liabilities

Chapter 10

The Bond Desk

Bonds. One iPad per desk; each desk runs a $1,000,000 bond fund for ten years. Each year three bonds hit the market: desks read the news, compare each bond's yield to its fair yield (Treasury rate plus the spread for its rating), and pass or buy $100K or $200K. You reveal what happened to each bond and why, then the standings. Has a Play without iPads mode. About 10 minutes a year.

 Chapter 10

The Underwriting Desk

Bonds. One iPad per team; each team is an underwriting desk pricing five companies' bonds. Each deal opens with market news (Treasury rate plus the rating spread sets the market rate). Teams call par, discount or premium, find n and i, pick the factors from PV tables, and enter the price; the first three right prices win the client's mandate. Entry checks, straight-line amortization, carrying value and a balance sheet follow, with a worked answer after every step, a slow 5th-to-1st finale, and a class results report. Has a Play without iPads mode. About 40 minutes.

Teams go to The Underwriting Desk on the site Host →
 Chapter 10

The Rating Room

Bond ratings. Team rounds with hidden lock-ins and the drumroll reveal, the 12-question blitz, and the leaderboard. Groups score each case file on the six-factor scorecard (payment history, earnings stability, debt load, interest coverage, cash cushion, backing and outlook) and turn the total into a rating from AAA to CCC. Runs on the projector only.

Chapter 9Current liabilities and warranties

Chapter 9

Paper Airplane Inc.

Warranty liabilities. Students join on their own devices and pick Team 1, Team 2, and so on. Everyone books the sale and forecasts the claims; each team's estimate is the average of its players. You call each plane: no warranty needed, repair, or replace, and every repair or replacement sends a journal entry to every player. Right entries earn team points, wrong ones cost points, and no names are shown. Estimate vs. actual, closest-estimate bonus, a Top 5 students podium, then team standings. About 40 to 50 minutes.
