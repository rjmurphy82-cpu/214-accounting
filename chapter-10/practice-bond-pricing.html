Bond Pricing Practice

Price a bond in 5 steps, then record it. Same 7 steps every time: interest, periods, PV of the principal, PV of the interest, price, issue entry, interest entry.

Keys: Space or → reveals the next part, then moves on. ← or Back hides the last part. H hides answers.
